# AI Business OS — Pricing Strategy v1.0

**Status:** Strategic foundation  
**Date:** 2026-06-28  
**Question this document answers:** *What do users pay for, and how do we charge fairly for value?*

**Related:** [Business Model](./BUSINESS_MODEL.md) · [Retention Model](./RETENTION_MODEL.md) · [Metrics](./METRICS.md)

---

## Pricing philosophy

### Users do not pay for AI

They do not buy:

- Tokens or API calls
- Agent runs or runtime minutes
- Model access or orchestration graphs
- "More intelligence"

Users pay for **business value**:

| They pay for | How they experience it |
| ------------ | ---------------------- |
| **Saved time** | First result in minutes, not hours of setup |
| **Better decisions** | Clear intent, prioritized next steps |
| **Completed work** | Deliverables saved to projects and history |
| **Business growth** | Repeatable progress on clients, revenue, content |

**Rule:** If pricing copy mentions AI, models, or agents, rewrite it.

---

## Value-based pricing principles

1. **Price the outcome layer** — goals, results, projects, continuity
2. **Align limits with jobs** — caps on results and projects, not on "messages"
3. **Upgrade after proof** — user feels value before hitting paywall
4. **Predictable bills** — no surprise token invoices
5. **Expansion follows habit** — charge more when the platform is already part of the week

## Packaging model (proposed)

### Tier structure

| Tier | Who | Core promise | Price anchor |
| ---- | --- | ------------ | ------------ |
| **Starter** | First-time operator | Your first business results | Free / trial |
| **Pro** | Active solo operator | Weekly progress, multiple projects | $29–49 / mo |
| **Business** | Small team | Shared projects, more capacity | $79–149 / mo |
| **Scale** | Growth business | Seats, priority, integrations | Custom |

*Exact price points require market testing. Ranges reflect SMB outcome software, not developer tools.*

### What each tier includes (value units)

| Capability | Starter | Pro | Business | Scale |
| ---------- | ------- | --- | -------- | ----- |
| Goals & intent flow | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Results per month | 5 | 30 | 100 | Custom |
| Active projects | 1 | 5 | 20 | Unlimited |
| History & continue | 7 days | Full | Full | Full |
| Team seats | 1 | 1 | 5 | Custom |
| Vertical packs | — | Add-on | Included | Custom |
| Export & share | Basic | Full | Full | Full |
| Priority processing | — | — | ✓ | ✓ |

### Overage philosophy

When users exceed limits **after demonstrated value**, offer:

- One-click upgrade (preferred)
- Small result pack ($X for 10 more results) — never punitive per-token billing

## Monetization moments

Charge or upgrade when value is already proven:

| Moment | User state | Offer |
| ------ | ---------- | ----- |
| **Post-first-result** | "This worked" | Trial → Pro with annual discount |
| **Third result in a week** | Habit forming | Pro features: more projects |
| **Project #2 created** | Organization need | Business tier |
| **Share / invite** | Collaboration need | Per-seat add-on |
| **Cap reached mid-month** | Active user | Upgrade with usage summary |

Never monetize before the first completed result.

## Free tier / trial design

### Purpose

Prove TTFV and trust — not host unlimited AI usage.

### Recommended trial shape

- **14 days** or **5 completed results**, whichever comes first
- Full UX including Wow, Intent, and Result — no degraded experience
- Clear countdown: "3 results left this month" (transparent, not scary)

### What free users should feel

> "I got real work done. I want to keep going."

Not:

> "I hit an AI limit."

## Pricing page messaging (user-facing)

### Headlines to use

- "Plans for businesses that want results, not more tools"
- "Pay for progress — clients found, plans finished, work completed"
- "Start free. Upgrade when the platform is part of your week."

### Headlines to avoid

- "More AI power"
- "Unlimited GPT access"
- "Advanced agent orchestration"

## Competitive positioning

| Category | Typical pricing | Our position |
| -------- | --------------- | ------------ |
| Chat AI ($20/mo) | Per seat, unlimited chat | We charge for **completed business work** |
| Automation ($50+/mo) | Per workflow / task | We charge for **outcomes**, setup is invisible |
| Agency services ($2K+/mo) | Human delivery | We are **fraction of cost** for repeatable jobs |
| Generic SaaS stacks | Per tool | We are **one OS** replacing scattered subscriptions |

## Discounts & annual plans

| Motion | Discount | Why |
| ------ | -------- | --- |
| Annual prepay | 15–20% | Improves retention and cash flow |
| Vertical launch | Time-limited | Beachhead acquisition |
| Referral credit | 1 month free | Growth loop (see Growth Engine) |
| Nonprofit / education | Case-by-case | Brand; not core GTM |

## Enterprise pricing (future)

- Per-seat + platform fee + minimum commitment
- Priced on **business outcomes under management** (projects, seats, regions)
- Professional services for rollout — separate SOW
