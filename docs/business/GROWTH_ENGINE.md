**Status:** Strategic foundation

## Growth thesis

AI Business OS grows when **users complete visible business work** and tell peers who have the same jobs-to-be-done. Growth is outcome-led: the product is the marketing when the first result is fast, trustworthy, and shareable.

Primary engine: **Product-led growth (PLG)** supported by content, community, referrals, and partners.

## Channel 1 — Organic growth (primary)

### Product-led discovery

| Mechanism | How it works |
| --------- | ------------ |
| **Shareable results** | User exports or shares deliverable (watermarked / link) |
| **Wow moment** | First 60s creates word-of-mouth: "You have to see this" |
| **SEO / content** | "How to get clients" articles → goal-aligned landing |
| **Template gallery** | Public examples of completed outcomes (future) |

### Organic principles

- Every shared artifact reinforces **outcome OS**, not "AI chat"
- Landing pages match goal language (Find Clients, Grow Revenue)
- Free trial lowers barrier; retention converts

### KPIs

- Organic signup % of total
- Share / export rate per result
- Branded link click-through

---

## Channel 2 — Referral loops

### Design

```text
User completes result → Prompt to invite peer
  → Peer signs up with referral code
    → Both get value (credit / extra results)
      → Peer completes first result → Loop repeats
```

### Referral rules

| Rule | Rationale |
| ---- | --------- |
| Reward **after first result** of invitee | Prevents low-quality signups |
| Credit in **business terms** (extra results, month free) | Aligns with value pricing |
| Cap abuse | Same domain / device limits |
| B2B language | "Invite a business partner" not "invite a friend" |

### Referral rewards (proposed)

| Party | Reward |
| ----- | ------ |
| Inviter | 1 month Pro credit or 10 bonus results |
| Invitee | Extended trial or bonus results |

### KPIs

- Referral participation rate (% of WAU who refer)
- Invitee activation rate (first result within 7 days)
- Referral CAC vs paid channels

## Channel 3 — Community

### Purpose

Turn vertical ICPs into **peer proof** and **habit reinforcement** — not a support forum for AI configuration.

### Community layers

| Layer | Format | Goal |
| ----- | ------ | ---- |
| **Vertical circles** | Brokers, consultants, MLM leaders | Share what worked |
| **Office hours** | Live goal → result demos | Trust + education |
| **Success stories** | User-written outcomes | Advocate stage fuel |
| **Ambassador program** | Power users with perks | Local evangelism |

### Community guardrails

- No "how to configure agents" culture
- Moderate toward **business outcomes**
- Official team reinforces invisible AI positioning

### KPIs

- Community-attributed signups
- Ambassador-led activations
- Story submissions per month

## Channel 4 — Content engine

### Content strategy

Content sells **jobs-to-be-done**, not product features.

| Content type | Example | Funnel stage |
| ------------ | ------- | ------------ |
| **Outcome guides** | "Client acquisition plan in 10 minutes" | Discover |
| **Vertical playbooks** | "Listing follow-up for brokers" | Understand |
| **Result showcases** | Redacted real deliverables | Trust |
| **Comparison** | "Outcome OS vs chat AI for business" | Understand |
| **Founder narrative** | "We built an OS for business progress" | Discover |

### Distribution

- SEO blog + LinkedIn (primary ICP channels)
- YouTube shorts: goal → result timelapse
- Newsletter: weekly "one goal to try"

### Production loop

```text
User result (anonymized) → Story → Article → Landing → Signup → Result
```

### KPIs

- Content → signup conversion
- Cost per activated user from content
- Top articles by first-result rate

| ------------ | ---------- |

### Partner enablement

- Partner dashboard: activations, revenue share

### KPIs

- Partner activation rate
- Revenue share as % of total

## Paid acquisition (secondary)

Use paid channels only when:

- LTV:CAC model validated on organic + referral cohorts
- Landing and TTFV are stable
- Unit economics work at **first-result activation**, not just signup

| Channel | Best for |
| ------- | -------- |
| LinkedIn | ICP 1–2 |
| Meta | Vertical beachheads |
| Google | High-intent JTBD keywords |

Target: **CAC payback < 6 months** on Pro tier.

## What we do not do

- Growth hacks that attract AI hobbyists
- Viral loops that expose internal architecture
- Discount-driven acquisition without first-result activation
- Marketplace-first GTM before core loop works

---

## North Star alignment

Growth is healthy when new users reach **first result** quickly and a growing share of **Weekly Active Businesses** come from **referral and organic** — not when signup volume alone increases.
