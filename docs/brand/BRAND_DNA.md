# Brand DNA — OSA v1

> **Status:** Brand Identity v1 — foundation  
> **Scope:** Defines what the OSA brand is and how it relates to AI Business OS  
> **Audience:** Design, product, engineering, sound, motion  
> **Does not contain:** Logo artwork, audio files, marketing slogans

---

## 1. What the OSA brand is

**OSA** is the brand layer of AI Business OS — the recognizable face of an operating system for business work.

| Layer | Role |
| ----- | ---- |
| **AI Business OS** | Product category and platform: where intentions become work and work becomes memory |
| **OSA** | Brand name and experience signature: calm, capable, invisible engine made felt through outcomes |
| **Orbit** | Primary brand mark: a geometric sign of focus, continuity, and return |

OSA is not a chatbot brand, not a model brand, and not a feature name users must learn. In user-facing surfaces the engine stays invisible; the **brand** expresses trust, continuity, and seriousness without exposing internal architecture.

Brand OSA answers three questions for the user:

1. *Is this serious business software or a toy?* — Serious, calm, structured.
2. *Will I have to learn AI jargon?* — No. Plain language, outcomes first.
3. *Will it remember me?* — Yes. Continuity is part of the identity.

---

## 2. Why the symbol is not a literal wasp

In Russian, **«оса»** means wasp. A literal insect illustration would:

- Fix the brand to a pun instead of a system
- Suggest aggression, sting, or danger — opposite of the product temperament
- Age quickly as illustration trends change
- Compete with the product promise of an invisible engine

The brand **borrows the acronym OSA**, not the animal. Users may never hear the letters spelled out in primary flows; the mark must work without entomology.

**Rule:** No wasp silhouettes, wings, stingers, hive metaphors, or yellow-black warning palettes as brand devices.

---

## 3. Orbit as the firm mark

**Orbit** is the canonical visual sign of OSA.

It represents:

| Idea | Meaning in product |
| ---- | ------------------ |
| **Center** | One current priority — not a dashboard of fifty tiles |
| **Orbit path** | Work in motion around a goal; projects, results, and memory circling a decision |
| **Return** | Coming back tomorrow without re-explaining; continuity |
| **Stability** | A system that holds course while the user runs the business |

Orbit is abstract geometry — arc, ring, or focal point with trajectory — never a mascot, never a letter monogram forced into a circle for its own sake.

Relationship to logo system: see [LOGO_SYSTEM.md](./LOGO_SYSTEM.md).

## 4. Brand temperament

| Dimension | OSA is | OSA is not |
| --------- | ------ | ---------- |
| Tone | Calm, direct, respectful of time | Hype, urgency theatre, gamified panic |
| Density | Airy, few decisions per screen | Control room, cockpit, dense admin |
| Intelligence | Assumed, never boasted | “Powered by AI”, model names, scores |
| Emotion | Quiet confidence | Cute mascot, jokes, meme tone |
| Authority | Partner in the business | Lecturing professor, magic trick |

---

## 5. Language principles

- **Russian** is the primary voice for first-touch and onboarding surfaces unless a surface is explicitly scoped otherwise.
- **Plain business Russian** — no internal codenames (Runtime, Orchestrator, Navigator) in primary copy.
- **Outcome verbs** — prepare, continue, save, finish — not configure, deploy, invoke.
- **No model or provider names** in user-facing brand touchpoints.

Voice welcome and in-product microcopy follow the same temperament: spoken or written, the brand sounds like a calm colleague opening a working session.

---

## 6. Relationship to product principles

Brand OSA must stay aligned with Product Bible constraints:

- Progress over intelligence
- Engine disappears
- Memory as relationship
- One primary action per first-time screen

When brand expression conflicts with a flashy demo or exposed machinery, **brand loses** — the product principle wins.

## 8. The Living Orbit

**Orbit** is the only interface element that may move on its own initiative.

Everything else waits for the user.

| Element | Motion rule |
| ------- | ----------- |
| **Orbit** | May animate independently — draw, breathe, complete an arc — when it signals system presence or progress |
| **Text, buttons, cards, navigation** | Appear on user action or on a single restrained entrance; never loop for decoration |
| **Background, chrome, secondary UI** | Static by default |

This is a brand law, not a visual preference. Autonomous motion is a privilege granted to one sign. If everything moves, nothing means progress. If only Orbit moves, motion becomes language.

Implications:

- Loading and “system is working” states may use Orbit motion with neutral copy — see [MOTION_SYSTEM.md](./MOTION_SYSTEM.md).
- Marketing and product UI must not introduce secondary mascots or icons that also self-animate.
- When Orbit is absent from a surface, **no element** replaces it as self-moving decoration.

Relationship to cinematic grammar: [MOTION_SYSTEM.md](./MOTION_SYSTEM.md) §10.

---

## 9. Decision checklist

Before shipping a branded touchpoint, confirm:

- [ ] No literal wasp or insect metaphor **in product UI or logo mark**
- [ ] Orbit used only per logo rules
- [ ] Only Orbit may self-animate — all other elements wait for the user
- [ ] No model/provider names visible
- [ ] No marketing slogan as headline
- [ ] Motion and sound optional, never autoplay by default
- [ ] Copy readable in five seconds
